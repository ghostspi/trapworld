HOW TO PLAY

  • MOVE — A / D or ← / →
  • JUMP — SPACE, W or ↑  ·  press again in mid-air to DOUBLE JUMP
  • RESTART — R  ·  PAUSE — ESC
  • MOBILE — on-screen buttons appear automatically
  • Reach the glowing portal to finish the level.
  • Checkpoints save your progress inside a level.
  • Everything is out to kill you. Learn the traps. Try again.
